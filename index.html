About Me

Software that runs the business, not just the screen

I'm a business software developer focused on one thing: building tools that real businesses use every single day — not demo software, not concept apps. My work centers on the systems that keep a business running smoothly behind the scenes: sales counters, stockrooms, supplier ledgers, and the numbers that tell an owner whether they made money this month.

I develop practical business management and POS software that helps companies manage sales, purchases, inventory, and accounts efficiently — replacing spreadsheets and paper registers with fast, reliable, database-driven systems that owners and staff can actually trust.

Every project starts with how the business already works, then builds the software around it — not the other way around.
